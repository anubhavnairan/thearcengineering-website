ABOUT THE ARC ENGINEERING

Engineering that connects design to delivery.

The Arc Engineering is a premier engineering firm based in Kannur, Kerala, specializing in comprehensive Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) contracting and consultancy services. Established in 2012, we deliver end-to-end engineering solutions tailored to commercial, residential, and industrial projects.

By bridging the gap between innovative design and practical execution, we ensure that every facility we engineer operates safely, efficiently, and sustainably. Our team of experienced engineers and project managers is dedicated to transforming complex blueprints into fully functional, high-performance infrastructures.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and innovative MEP engineering partner in the region, recognized for delivering future-ready, sustainable infrastructure solutions that stand the test of time.

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled contracting and consultancy services by prioritizing technical excellence, operational safety, and client-centric project management, ensuring seamless execution from concept to handover.
